Noisedeck Sync for Linux uses OpenSSL and libuv through their system-provided
shared libraries. Their licenses and source information are provided by the
Ubuntu packages installed on the system.

The optional NDI runtime is discovered dynamically. It is not linked into or
redistributed with Noisedeck Sync and remains subject to Vizrt's terms.

The v4l2loopback kernel module and utilities are installed from Ubuntu's
packages. Noisedeck Sync does not redistribute a kernel module or fork.
